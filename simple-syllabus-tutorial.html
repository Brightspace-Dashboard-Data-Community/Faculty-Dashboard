Simple Syllabus walkthrough

For new faculty: add the Syllabus link to your course navbar, then update and release your syllabus in Simple Syllabus. About 15–20 minutes the first time.

What this covers

Part 1 switches your course to the ELC Nav - Grand - with Simple Syllabus navbar so students see a Syllabus link. Part 2 walks through editing required fields and using Release to Students in Simple Syllabus.

In this tutorial

  1. Add Syllabus to your course navbar
  2. Update your syllabus in Simple Syllabus
  3. Preview and release to students
  4. Quick checklist

1. Add Syllabus to your course navbar

About 5 minutes. Do this once per course (or after copying a shell that does not already include Simple Syllabus).

  1. Open the course in D2L Brightspace and go to Course Home.
  2. On the dark course navbar, click the ⋯ (three-dot) button on the far right.
  3. Choose Manage All Course Navbars.
  1. On the Navbars page, open the Active Navbar dropdown.
  2. Select ELC Nav - Grand - with Simple Syllabus.
  3. Click Apply.
  1. Return to Course Home. Confirm that Syllabus now appears on the navbar (typically near Course Home / Content).

Tip: If you do not see the Simple Syllabus navbar in the list, contact elearning@example.edu. Do not create a custom navbar unless eLearning asks you to.

2. Update your syllabus in Simple Syllabus

About 10–15 minutes. Fill required course-specific fields and review editable sections. Institutional policy blocks are often locked.

  1. From your course navbar, click Syllabus to open Simple Syllabus.
  2. Confirm you are in the correct course offering. The header shows the term/section and status (for example, Version 1 - In Progress).
  3. Complete all required fields (shown in red until finished). Common examples: Office and Student Office Hours under Instructor Information.
  1. Work through the remaining sections (technology, materials, assignments, grading, schedule, course-specific policies, and so on). Use the rich text editor to add or revise your course details.
  2. For each section, review:
    • Visible — whether the section appears on the syllabus. Locked sections with a padlock are required by the college and cannot be hidden.
    • Public — whether the section appears on the public-facing syllabus site. Move to Private if the content should be available only to enrolled students.
  3. Use sidebar guidance on the right of each block for what to include. For current college policy wording you can copy into editable sections, see Simple Syllabus policy updates.

Note: Some blocks (for example Drop/Withdrawal, Disability Resources, Title IX) are institutional content. Edit only what the interface allows; leave locked or college-managed text as provided.

3. Preview and release to students

About 2–5 minutes. Release is blocked until every required component is complete.

  1. At the bottom of the Simple Syllabus editor, click Preview to review the student view.
  2. If the green status message says the syllabus cannot be submitted, click it for a list of unfinished required components and finish those sections.
  3. When validation is clear, click Release to Students.
  4. Confirm the syllabus status is no longer In Progress, then open the Syllabus navbar link again to verify what students will see.

Important: Releasing publishes the syllabus for learners in this course offering. If you edit after release, follow Simple Syllabus prompts to save and re-release (or publish the new version) so students see your updates.

4. Quick checklist

Use this at the start of each term for every course shell.

  • Active navbar is ELC Nav - Grand - with Simple Syllabus
  • Syllabus link appears on the course navbar
  • Instructor Information required fields are complete (office, student office hours, and any other red prompts)
  • Course-specific sections (materials, grading, policies, schedule) are filled in
  • Visible / Public toggles reviewed for sections you control
  • Preview looks correct
  • Release to Students completed for the current term
  • Simple Syllabus policy updates (copy current institutional wording)
  • Semester reminders
