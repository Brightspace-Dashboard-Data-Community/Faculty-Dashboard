Where to put your feedback

Sticky notes on PDF submissions are not captured. Put substantive feedback in rubric criterion rows or the overall feedback box so it can be analyzed here.

If you need to point to a spot in a document, use a numbered sticky note for location only, and write the actual feedback in the rubric or overall box.

Local processing & privacy

Analysis runs in your browser — grade comments are not sent to an external AI server. Use the training privacy mask (avatar menu or Alt+Shift+P) before sharing or projecting. Do not share screenshots or PDFs without authorization.
