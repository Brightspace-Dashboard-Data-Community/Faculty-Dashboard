Google Slides

Layouts, slide titles, and object order affect how assistive technology reads your deck.

Layouts & titles

  • Choose accessible themes and layouts that include a title placeholder.
  • Give every slide a unique title visible in the outline.

Reading order

  • Open Arrange → Order (or the accessibility side panel) to review how elements are read.
  • Place content in a logical sequence matching how you want students to hear it.

Images & video

  • Add alt text to meaningful images.
  • Embed video with captions when you link or embed from supported hosts.
