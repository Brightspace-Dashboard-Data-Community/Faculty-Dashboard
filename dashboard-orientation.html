1. How to use this dashboard

The Faculty Dashboard is a Brightspace-connected hub built by the eLearning Office. It does not replace your course shell — it sits beside it.

  • Use the left sidebar to move between sections. The hamburger icon collapses or expands the menu.
  • Your name appears at the bottom of the sidebar when you are signed in through Brightspace.
  • Many pages read live course and enrollment data from Brightspace using your session — stay signed in.
  • FERPA: student names, grades, and discussion content may appear. Do not share screenshots or exports without authorization.
  • On deeper pages, look for a green ← Back to … link above the title to return to the parent section.

2. Home

Orientation only — a short map of the dashboard, not your live grade or activity numbers.

  1. Open Home to see what the dashboard is for and jump links into each major area.
  2. Use Go to My Courses when you are ready for semester metrics and your course list.
  3. Use Browse Tools & Reports when you need merge, sandbox, agents, exports, or audits.

Open Home

3. My Courses

Your working overview for the current term.

  1. Open My Courses.
  2. Review the welcome card and summary metrics (courses, students, ungraded items, and related signals).
  3. Optionally use Filter by Course to focus the lower metrics on one offering.
  4. Scroll to your course list to open offerings, including courses pinned on your Brightspace homepage.
  5. Use Quick Tools on this page for frequent actions (same destination pages linked from elsewhere in D2L).
  6. For walkthroughs and written guides, open Faculty Tutorials from the sidebar or the link under Quick Tools.

Open My Courses

4. Students

Student-focused views for outreach and follow-up — separate from course setup tools.

  1. Open Students from the sidebar.
  2. Use the widgets there to review activity and risk-style signals across your courses.
  3. For accommodations, LDAA lookups, and related utilities, also see User Management under Tools & Reports.

Open Students

5. Analytics

Deeper course-health and activity views. Expand Analytics in the sidebar to see the submenu.

  • Course Health — engagement, assessment, and friction-style signals.
  • Assignments — completion and grading patterns.
  • Discussions — overview, plus 1-on-1 Activity for private conversation tracking.
  • Quizzes — attempt and score patterns (drill into a quiz for detail).

Open Course Health Analytics

6. Resources

Faculty-facing reference cards and semester communications — not live course data.

  1. Open Resources.
  2. Browse cards for semester-start reminders, dashboard notes, and related faculty materials.
  3. Copy text when you need it for email or announcements.

Open Resources

7. Faculty Training

Tutorials and guided practice for Brightspace and dashboard tasks.

  1. Open Faculty Training.
  2. Start with highlighted walkthroughs such as the AI Policy, Simple Syllabus, or Accessibility.
  3. Use the module lists and learning paths for broader D2L skill building.

Open Faculty Training

8. Accessibility

One hub for making course materials usable for every learner.

  1. Open Accessibility.
  2. Use format guides (Word, PDF, PowerPoint, Google docs/sheets/slides, multimedia, email, D2L webpages).
  3. Open YuJa Panorama training from the Accessibility hub when you need tool-specific practice.

Open Accessibility

9. Forms

Request and process forms that support course and enrollment workflows.

  1. Open Forms.
  2. Choose the form that matches your request (for example faculty course adds).
  3. Submit through the linked workflow; contact the Help Desk if you are unsure which form to use.

Open Forms

10. Tools & Reports

Action utilities and operational reports. Expand Tools & Reports in the sidebar, or start from the overview.

  • Overview — counts of tools vs reports in each area.
  • Course Management — merge, rollover kit, sandbox, due dates, agents, private conversations, course reports, and related items.
  • User Management — accommodations, LDAA lookup, and enrollment-related utilities.
  • Analytics & Reports — analytics launches plus operational reports such as Intelligent Agent Reply-To settings.

Each hub page splits items into Tools (things you do) and Reports (things you audit or export).

Open Tools & Reports

11. Updates

Release notes for Brightspace and dashboard changes. The sidebar badge marks when something new is available.

  1. Open Updates.
  2. Filter by feature, improvement, or other categories as needed.
  3. Skim current-term items first; older notes remain for reference.

Open Updates

12. Suggested next steps

A practical path for the first visit this term.

  1. Open My Courses and confirm your current-term offerings look right.
  2. When you are ready for the next term, work through the Semester Rollover Kit.
  3. If you teach multiple sections that should share one shell, use the Course Merge Form (same link used elsewhere in D2L).
  4. Add Simple Syllabus to your navbar with the Simple Syllabus walkthrough.
  5. Bookmark Tools & Reports for sandbox, agents, accommodations, and exports.
  6. Need help? Submit a ticket through the Help Desk Ticketing System.
