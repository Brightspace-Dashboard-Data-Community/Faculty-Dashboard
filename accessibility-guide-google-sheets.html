Google Sheets

Name sheets clearly, structure tables, and describe charts for screen reader users.

Structure

  • Rename tabs with short, meaningful titles.
  • Turn data ranges into a table with header row when appropriate.
  • Keep one coherent table per area; avoid blank rows that split related data unexpectedly.

Content

  • Add alt text to charts and images.
  • Summarize important chart takeaways in adjacent cells or a notes section.
  • Use sufficient color contrast and do not rely on color alone for status or grades.
