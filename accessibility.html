Platform change — July 2026

Your Institution switched from Ally to YuJa Panorama in July 2026. Use the accessibility indicator next to files in Content to open Panorama reports, remediate issues, and give students alternative formats. Ally indicators and workflows are no longer used.

YuJa Panorama scans course files and HTML pages, scores accessibility against WCAG-aligned checks, guides you through fixes (including AI-assisted options), and automatically generates alternative formats for students. Start with the training below, then use the format checklists when you author new materials.

YuJa Panorama in D2L

Panorama is integrated into your course Content area. Look for the accessibility icon or score next to documents and pages.

  • Accessibility reports & scores

    Open a file’s Accessibility Report to see its score, review individual issues, and follow Panorama’s guidance on how to fix each one.

  • Remediation tools

    Fix issues with Guided Remediation, AutoPilot AI suggestions, Structural Remediation (reading order and tagging), or by requesting help from YuJa Accessibility Experts when available.

  • Alternative formats for students

    Students can download formats such as audio, EPUB, braille, Immersive Reader, OCR outputs, math formats, and language translations—generated automatically without extra setup from you.

  • WCAG-aligned checklist

    Panorama checks PDFs, Office files, HTML, and more for issues like missing titles, alt text, headings, contrast, tables, and captions. See our training for the most common faculty-facing checks.

Quick start in D2L

  1. Open your course and go to Content.
  2. Find a document or page and click its accessibility icon (or score) to open the Accessibility Report.
  3. Select View All Issues to see what affects the score, then Fix Issue for guidance on each item.
  4. Choose Fix This Document and pick Guided Remediation, AutoPilot, or Structural Remediation as needed.
  5. When finished, apply fixes so the updated file replaces the original in your course.

Other accessibility tools in D2L

Use these alongside Panorama when you build HTML content and media.

  • Alt text for images

    When adding images through the HTML Editor in D2L, add alt text so visual content is available to screen reader users.

  • D2L Accessibility Checker

    Scan HTML content in the Brightspace editor for potential issues before you publish a topic or page.

  • Captions & audio descriptions

    Provide captions for video and narrate key visual elements when they are essential to understanding the content.

  • ReadSpeaker

    Text-to-speech and reading tools for listening to documents and HTML pages in your D2L courses.

Faculty training (YuJa Panorama)

Self-paced tutorials written for Your Institution faculty. Completion times are estimates.

  • Open Accessibility Reports in D2L — 5–10 min. Find the accessibility icon, open a report, and read your score and issue list.
  • Guided Remediation — 15–20 min. Fix issues one at a time with Panorama’s suggestions and apply changes back to your course file.
  • AutoPilot AI remediation — 10–15 min. Let Panorama propose AI-assisted fixes for supported file types, then review and apply.
  • Alternative formats for students — 5–10 min. Preview formats students can use and know where the student toggle lives.
  • Common issues checklist — 10–15 min. Know the high-impact checks Panorama runs on PDFs, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, and HTML.
