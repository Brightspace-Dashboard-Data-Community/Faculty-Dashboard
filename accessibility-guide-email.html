Email

Course announcements often start as email; plain structure and meaningful links help every recipient.

Structure & clarity

  • Use a clear subject line that states the purpose and deadline if applicable.
  • Put the most important information first; use short paragraphs and real bullet lists.
  • Use heading styles in Outlook HTML messages when available, or clear bold lead-ins for sections.

Links & attachments

  • Use descriptive link text instead of pasting long raw URLs as the only label.
  • If you attach PDFs or Office files, ensure they meet the same accessibility standards as files in D2L.

Images & signatures

  • Add alt text to informative images in the signature or body.
  • Do not rely on image-only banners for critical dates or instructions—repeat key text in the message body.
