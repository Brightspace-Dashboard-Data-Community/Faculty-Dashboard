Term Rollover Assistant

Copy selected materials from a past course into your upcoming offering, add new items, then edit dates, rename, or delete.

How rollover works
  1. Choose the upcoming course (destination), then the past course (source).
  2. Uncheck items you do not want, and optionally Add new assignments, quizzes, or discussions.
  3. Click Copy into upcoming course. Brightspace copies full packages first (quizzes keep questions). Items you left unchecked are then removed from the upcoming course.
  4. In step 4, update dates, rename, delete, or add more items as needed.
