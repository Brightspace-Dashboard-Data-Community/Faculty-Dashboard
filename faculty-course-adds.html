Add users to your courses with appropriate roles

Add User to Course

This tool allows an Instructor to add users to courses where they have Instructor access. You can add users with roles such as Course Evaluator, Secondary Instructor, Copy Course, Teaching Assistant, SLA, or CSC.

If you need assistance with this tool, please contact the eLearning Office through the Help Desk Ticketing System.

Search by name, username, or campus email. Partial first names (for example Jon for Jonathan) are supported. The person does not need to be in the selected course already.
See the role descriptions below. Contact the eLearning Office if you need assistance.

Understanding course roles

When adding someone to a course, choose the role that matches their responsibility. Role names and IDs are defined by your institution in D2L. D2L Valence API – Users & Roles

Secondary Instructor
Same permissions as Instructor but does not appear on the Student Evaluation Form. Use for co-teachers or support instructors.
Course Evaluator
Instructor or Division Chair role for reviewing a course for promotion or other evaluation purposes.
Teaching Assistant
Can view all content but cannot make changes. Read-only access to support teaching.
CSC (Course Success Coach)
Course Success Coaches can view student information, send emails, and post content under Content. This role often replaces SLA and is commonly used by TLC.
Librarian
Role for library staff to access the course and support students with resources.
Copy Course
Instructor-style role used to copy a course into the enrollee’s own course offering.
