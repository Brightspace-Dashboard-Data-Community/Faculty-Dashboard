Multimedia (audio, video, captioning)

Learners need equivalent access to spoken content, sound effects, and important visuals.

Video

  • Provide accurate closed captions synchronized with speech and meaningful sounds.
  • Offer a transcript when it helps students search, translate, or review offline.
  • When critical information appears only on screen (demos, equations, speaker names on screen), add audio description in narration or a separate track.
  • Avoid rapid flashing that could trigger photosensitivity; use player controls that are keyboard accessible.

Audio-only

  • Supply a text transcript for podcasts and voice-only lectures.

Hosting in D2L

  • Use platforms your institution supports (Kaltura, YouTube with captions, etc.) and embed with captions turned on by default when possible.
  • Check that the embedded player is operable with a keyboard and that volume and pause controls are available.
